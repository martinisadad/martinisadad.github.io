The Core Framework

The research introduces two key concepts to systematically map the impact of AI: the Human Agency Scale (HAS) to quantify worker preference, and the Desire-Capability Landscape to classify tasks for strategic action.

When asked about their preferred level of interaction, workers overwhelmingly favor a collaborative partnership with AI (H3) over full automation (H1) or full manual control (H5). They want a "copilot," not a replacement.

From Copilot to Autopilot

The industry is rapidly moving beyond simple AI "copilots" that suggest code snippets to autonomous "agents" capable of managing entire development cycles. This represents a fundamental shift in the human-AI relationship within software engineering.

"The era of the programmer as a mere 'coder' is over. The prompt is the new source code. The new job is to be the architect, the prompter, the teacher, and the orchestrator of AI systems." — Andrej Karpathy, Y Combinator AI Startup School in June 2025

This shift directly challenges the professional identity of many engineers. While few enjoy writing boilerplate code (a "Green Light" task), many derive immense satisfaction and a sense of craftsmanship from complex problem-solving and system architecture. As AI agents become more capable, they begin to encroach on these core, high-value tasks, moving them into the contentious "Red Light" zone where AI is capable but human desire to retain control is high. This creates a new "great mismatch" within the very industry that is building this technology.

The Emerging Skill Premium

As AI masters routine information processing, the economic value of human skills is shifting. The data suggests a decline in the premium for analytical tasks and a rise in the importance of uniquely human interpersonal and organizational competencies.

Value is Diminishing

These skills are increasingly automatable and are less prominent in tasks requiring high human agency.

  • ✗Analyzing data or information
  • ✗Processing information
  • ✗Updating and using relevant knowledge
  • ✗Documenting/recording information

Value is Ascending

These interpersonal and organizational skills are frequently associated with tasks where human agency is essential.

  • ✓Guiding, directing, and motivating subordinates
  • ✓Coordinating the work and activities of others
  • ✓Developing and building teams
  • ✓Establishing and maintaining interpersonal relationships
